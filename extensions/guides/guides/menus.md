# Menus, and the buttons on screen


Every **⋯** opens that thing's menu: beside a floor's sign, on a worker's row and its head, and on a file or page a worker showed. A right-click, or the context-menu key, opens the same menu. It lists everything you can do with that floor, worker or showing, and every extension's action on it.

On a worker's head, anything that doesn't fit is listed first in its ⋯, and **Pin to the head** keeps an action or a line in place. A worker's row has **Pin to the row**, and a showing's tab has **Pin to the tab**.

Once you know where things are, you can clear buttons off the page. In **Settings** (the gear at the top of the sidebar), **Buttons on screen** sets each group to **Shown** or **Menu**: the extensions' buttons on a floor or a worker, a floor's directory buttons and trays, a worker's own Resume, Let go and Send home. The same choice sits at the end of each **Pin to…** menu. A group set to Menu keeps whatever you pinned there, and nothing leaves the tower: every button is still in its ⋯ and in the palette (**⌘K**).

**Try it:** open the ⋯ on one of your workers, then open Settings and set a group to Menu.
