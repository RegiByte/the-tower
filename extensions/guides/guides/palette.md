# The command palette

**⌘K** opens the command palette anywhere in the tower page, inside a terminal too. It is the fastest way to anything.

Type to:

- **go** to a worker (by its callsign, its task or its floor), a floor, a shelf entry or a draft;
- **run** a command, shown with its key;
- **do** what the selected worker and the floors offer: resume, review, send home, a new worker or shell, Tidy.

↑ and ↓ move, **Enter** picks, **Esc** closes. A pick runs exactly what its button runs: one that can't be taken back asks first, and one that needs words, such as a prompt or a note, opens the form that takes them.

With nothing typed, it opens on what the selected worker offers, who waits on you and what you picked lately. Below them is everything else, last of all what the tower can teach.

**Try it:** press ⌘K and type the name of one of your floors.

The config's `keys` rebinds ⌘K as `palette`; `?` lists every key.
