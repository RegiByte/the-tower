# Field notes

A week of notes from the lighthouse on the north cape, kept for the keeper who comes next.

## Monday

Fog until noon, then a clear sky and a long swell from the west. The lamp ran all night without a fault, and the
gulls were back on the gallery rail by morning.

## Tuesday

The supply boat came early, with oil, bread and a crate of oranges nobody ordered. We moved the logbooks to the dry
shelf in the store room and patched the window on the stairs.

## Wednesday

Wind from the north, strong enough to hum in the railings. Nothing passed the cape all day but a pair of seals, who
watched the tower for an hour and then went back to sleep on the rocks.
