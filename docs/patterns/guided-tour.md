# One user giving another a tour

A user who knows the tower walks a new one through it on the newcomer's own floors.

Rafael felt lost in the tower for his first days: lots of panels, no idea which mattered. Ana sat with him for
twenty minutes and showed him three things on his own floor: who was waiting on him and the key that goes there,
a worker's Changes and review thread, and hiring a worker from a sentence.

After that he found the rest on his own. A worker can give the same tour: ask one “give me a tour of the tower”,
and it reads your floors and walks you through what fits them.
